About Aotearoa Adventure Gear

We are a small crew of trampers, climbers and packrafters based in Ōtautahi Christchurch. Every item we stock is carried, pitched, soaked and repaired somewhere in this country before it reaches the shelf - if it cannot handle a Southern Alps nor'wester, we do not sell it.
